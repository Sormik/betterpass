# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else, is expected to:
- be friendly, patient and respectful, whatever someone's experience, background or identity,
- give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the project and its users,
- take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Not acceptable are, among other things:
- insults, harassment, discriminating or sexualized language and images,
- personal or political attacks, trolling,
- publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission,
- any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (repository owner Sormik). Reports are handled confidentially.
